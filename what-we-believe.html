THE FOUNDATION OF OUR FAITH

Built on the Word of God

We believe the Bible is God's Word and the foundation for our faith, teaching, and daily living. It guides us in knowing God, following Jesus Christ, and serving others with humility and love.

The beliefs below are a sample statement of faith for website planning. Please review them with your church leadership and adjust them to match your congregation's officially approved doctrine before publishing.

WHAT WE STAND FOR

Our Statement of Faith

These sample statements summarize central Christian beliefs. Scripture references are provided for further study.

SCRIPTURE

The Bible

We believe the Bible is the inspired Word of God and our trustworthy guide for faith, truth, and godly living.

2 Timothy 3:16–17 · Psalm 119:105
GOD

One True God

We believe in one true God, the Creator of heaven and earth, who reveals Himself as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

Deuteronomy 6:4 · Matthew 28:19
JESUS CHRIST

Jesus Christ

We believe Jesus Christ is the Son of God, who came into the world, died for our sins, rose again, and is Lord and Savior.

John 3:16 · 1 Corinthians 15:3–4
SALVATION

Salvation by Grace

We believe salvation is God's gift of grace, received through faith in Jesus Christ, not earned by human works.

Ephesians 2:8–9 · Romans 10:9–10
HOLY SPIRIT

The Holy Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit works in believers to guide, strengthen, transform, and empower them to live for God and serve others.

John 14:26 · Acts 1:8
THE CHURCH

The Church

We believe the Church is the body of Christ, called to worship God, build up believers, share the Gospel, and make disciples of all nations.

Matthew 28:19–20 · Hebrews 10:24–25
CHRISTIAN LIVING

A Life of Faith and Love

We believe followers of Jesus are called to grow in holiness, love their neighbors, serve faithfully, and reflect Christ in their daily lives.

Micah 6:8 · John 13:34–35
THE RETURN OF JESUS CHRIST

The Return of Jesus

We believe Jesus Christ will return as He promised, and we seek to live faithfully while awaiting His return.

John 14:2–3 · 1 Thessalonians 4:16–17
