The Tithe

The Tithe is the 10% of our income that we set apart for the Lord. It belongs to Him, and giving it is an act of worship, obedience, and faithfulness. As we honor God with our tithes, we acknowledge that everything we have comes from Him. These tithes are entrusted to the church to support the work and needs of His house.

Malachi 3:10

Missions & Outreach

The Next Level fund supports our church's outreaches, ministries, and missions. It helps provide resources for community outreach, ministry activities, evangelism, and mission efforts. Through your generosity, we can reach more people, serve our community, strengthen our ministries, and share the Gospel beyond the walls of our church.
