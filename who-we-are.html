WELCOME TO OUR CHURCH

A Family United by Faith

Family of Faith Missions, Inc. is a church community committed to growing in faith, building meaningful relationships, and sharing God's love.

We believe that the church is more than a place to gather. It is a family where people can worship together, encourage one another, and grow in their walk with God.

OUR PURPOSE & DIRECTION

Our Mission & Vision

Guided by faith, united in purpose, and committed to sharing God's love.

Our Mission

To proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ, win families and communities to Him, nurture believers through biblical teaching and discipleship, and equip and send faithful servants to fulfill the Great Commission through evangelism, missions, and compassionate service.

Our Vision

To see families transformed by the Gospel of Jesus Christ, believers rooted in God's Word, and communities reached through the love and power of Christ, for the glory of God.
